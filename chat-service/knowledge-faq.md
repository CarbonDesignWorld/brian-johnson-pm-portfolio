=== SOURCE: FAQ written by Brian (/contact) ===

Q: Why is Brian moving from sales into product management?
A: Brian spent about a decade selling software at Notion, Bravely, Bonusly and Autodesk, close to the customer problems that products are built to solve. He then trained full-time in product design, management and AI and began building products himself. Product management joins both halves: understanding what customers will pay for, and shipping it.

Q: What PM experience does he have without a PM title?
A: Brian has led four products end to end, from research and prioritization through to launch or handoff. He shipped Prepare v0.1 and tested it with 20 research participants who used it daily. He took C.O.T from research to a handed-off MVP in six weeks. Each case study shows the trade-offs he made and what he cut.

Q: Is Brian technical?
A: He designs in Figma and builds working products himself. Prepare is a live web app he built with React, TypeScript and Gemini and deployed on Google Cloud Run. He works comfortably with engineers because he has shipped code, not just specs.

Q: How does he use AI in his work?
A: He uses AI where it's safe and keeps rules in charge where accuracy matters. In Prepare, a rules engine decides what to wear and Gemini only writes the words. For Just Hair, he chose to ground an existing model in curated hair-care knowledge rather than train one.

Q: How does he prioritize?
A: He ranks user needs, checks them against business goals, and cuts anything that doesn't serve the core loop. At C.O.T he shipped the top two of six research needs. He deferred gamification despite strong interest, because booking and payment came first.

Q: What does his sales background add?
A: Comfort with customers, revenue and stakeholders. He leads with the business case and justifies the segment before the solution, and he's used to saying no.

Q: What kind of role is he looking for?
A: Product Manager roles, ideally 0-to-1 or early-stage products. He's based in New York City and is open to remote, hybrid and on-site roles.

Q: How can I talk to Brian?
A: Book a 15-minute intro or a 30-minute product conversation from the Meetings icon, or email bjohnson1041@gmail.com.

Q: How was this site built?
A: Brian designed it in a Notion-inspired style and built it with Astro and React, with this assistant running on Gemini Flash through a small Google Cloud Run service.
